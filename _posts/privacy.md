## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe fine art galleries should be enjoyed with total peace of mind. When browsing Alien Studio:
- We do not set behavioral advertising or marketing tracking cookies.
- We do not profile visitors or sell your browsing data to third parties.
- We do not perform canvas fingerprinting or cross-site tracking.

## 2. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you submit an inquiry through our Contact Form (for gallery exhibitions, print acquisitions, or licensing), we collect:
- **Your Name:** To address you in our correspondence.
- **Your Email Address:** To reply to your inquiry.
- **Subject & Message Details:** To process and fulfill your request.

## 3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Privacy Standards

Alien Studio works exclusively with privacy-conscious service providers:
- **Formspree:** Contact form submissions are processed securely via Formspree's encrypted transmission endpoint under strict data processing agreements.
- **CloudCDN & Edge Distribution:** Assets are served via privacy-hardened edge caching with Subresource Integrity (SRI) verification to protect against tampering.
- **Etsy Storefront:** External links to our verified Etsy shop ([AlienStudioShop ⧉](https://www.etsy.com/shop/AlienStudioShop)) are governed by Etsy's independent privacy policy upon navigating to their domain.

## 4. Your Legal Rights (UK / EU GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:
- **Right of Access:** Request a copy of any personal communications we hold.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request immediate permanent deletion of your inquiry messages.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Right to Object & Restrict Processing:** Withdraw consent for communication at any time.

## 5. Contact Our Privacy Lead

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact our team at [contact@alienstudio.com](mailto:contact@alienstudio.com).
